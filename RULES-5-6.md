# Five- and six-player rules

This deployment uses the current paired-player rules, not the obsolete special building phase.

Source: [CATAN 2025 5–6 player rulebook](https://www.catan.com/sites/default/files/2025-03/CN3082%20CATAN%20%E2%80%93%205-6%20Rulebook%202025%20reduced.pdf), especially pages 1, 3 and 4. [Official paired-turn clarification](https://www.catan.com/sites/default/files/2021-09/CATAN_New5-6Player_ruleEN.pdf).

- Five or six connected players automatically select the 30-hex island, with two deserts, 11 harbours, the extension's number-disc supply and A–Zc spiral placement. Harbours use the randomized setup option.
- The supply contains 24 of each resource, 20 Knights, 5 VP cards and 3 each of Road Building, Year of Plenty (Invention) and Monopoly. Each player keeps the normal piece limits.
- Opening dice select the starting player; ties reroll. Settlement/road placement proceeds forward then backward. Starting resources come from each player's second settlement.
- Player 1 rolls, resolves production or a seven, and takes the ordinary trade/build turn. Player 2 is the player three seats ahead and acts only after Player 1 finishes.
- Player 2 may build, buy/play development cards and use the bank/harbours. Player 2 cannot roll again or trade with players. They may respond to Player 1's trade offers during Player 1's portion, like any other player.
- After Player 2 finishes, both role markers advance one seat.
- Each portion permits one development card, excluding cards bought during that portion. Cards bought in an earlier Player 1/2 portion are available in the next portion that player receives.
- More than seven resource cards still requires discarding half on a seven. Either player may win in their own portion at ten points; Player 1's win ends the game before Player 2 acts.

New games with two to four players retain the existing base-game turn system. Saved base games remain compatible and are not converted into extension games.
